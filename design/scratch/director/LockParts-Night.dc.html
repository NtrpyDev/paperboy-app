Key idea

The first direct detection of gravitational waves, and the first sight of two black holes merging.

The merger happened about 410 megaparsecs away, roughly 1.3 billion light years.† The signal reached Livingston about 7 milliseconds before Hanford.

† The distance carries a wide error range, roughly 230 to 570 megaparsecs. The sky position was known only to within about 600 square degrees.
