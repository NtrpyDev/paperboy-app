Both detectors recorded the same chirp on September 14, 2015. It reached Livingston about 7 milliseconds before Hanford. Its shape matches two black holes of about 36 and 29 solar masses spiraling into one of about 62 solar masses.

By the time the wave reached Earth, its peak strain was \(1.0 \times 10^{-21}\). Before and after, the masses add up, each within its error range:

\[ 36\,M_\odot + 29\,M_\odot \approx 62\,M_\odot + 3\,M_\odot \tag*{Eq. 1} \]

Here \(M_\odot\) is one solar mass. About \(3\,M_\odot\) left as gravitational waves. xxxx \(x\)
