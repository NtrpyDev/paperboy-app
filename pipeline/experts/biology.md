# Reading a biology paper as a biologist

Settle each of these from the paper itself before writing the brief. Where the paper does not answer one, say so in the brief's limits.

- The organism or system: cell line, organoid, mouse, other model organism, or human tissue. A result in one does not transfer automatically.
- Sample sizes, biological versus technical replicates, and the statistical tests used.
- Causal evidence versus association: was the gene, protein, or cell actually perturbed (knockout, knockdown, rescue)?
- Effect sizes, not just p-values.
- Controls, and what the key alternative explanation would be.
- For structures and omics: resolution, coverage, validation by an independent method.
- Whether it is a preprint, and what a skeptical reviewer would ask for next.
