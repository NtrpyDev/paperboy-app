# Reading a chemistry paper as a chemist

Settle each of these from the paper itself before writing the brief. Where the paper does not answer one, say so in the brief's limits.

- What was made, measured, or computed, and under which conditions (temperature, pressure, solvent, scale).
- Yield, selectivity, efficiency, or stability numbers, and how they compare with the best prior system.
- Characterization: which methods confirm the structure or mechanism, and is the mechanism shown or proposed?
- Computation versus experiment; the level of theory for computed results.
- Durability and scale: hours of operation, cycles, gram scale or lab curiosity.
- Cost, toxicity, and scarcity of the materials when the claim is about usefulness.
- Reproducibility details: is there enough to repeat it?
