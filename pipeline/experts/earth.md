# Reading an earth science paper as an earth and climate scientist

Settle each of these from the paper itself before writing the brief. Where the paper does not answer one, say so in the brief's limits.

- Observation, reconstruction from proxies, or model projection? Keep them distinct.
- The region and time span covered, and the spatial and temporal resolution.
- Uncertainty ranges and what drives them; ensemble spread for model results.
- For projections: which scenarios and assumptions.
- Attribution claims: how natural variability was separated from the signal.
- Data sources and their known biases or gaps.
- Units and baselines: a change relative to which period?
