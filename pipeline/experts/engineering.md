# Reading a engineering paper as an engineer

Settle each of these from the paper itself before writing the brief. Where the paper does not answer one, say so in the brief's limits.

- The device, material, or system and the headline metric, with units.
- Test conditions versus real operating conditions; lab prototype or deployed system?
- Comparison with the current state of the art at the same conditions.
- Durability: cycles, hours, degradation.
- Efficiency accounting: what is included in and excluded from the number.
- Scale and manufacturability: what stands between this and practical use.
- Safety and failure modes when relevant.
