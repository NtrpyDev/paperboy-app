# Reading a medicine paper as a clinical researcher

Settle each of these from the paper itself before writing the brief. Where the paper does not answer one, say so in the brief's limits.

- Study design: randomized trial, cohort, case-control, case series, or meta-analysis. Say which, plainly.
- Who was studied: number of people, ages, condition, where; who was excluded.
- The primary endpoint and whether it is a clinical outcome or a surrogate marker.
- Absolute risk and number needed to treat alongside any relative risk.
- Confidence intervals, follow-up length, dropouts, and whether the trial was preregistered.
- Harms and side effects, reported with the same care as benefits.
- Confounding in observational work, funding and conflicts of interest, and the plain statement that this is not medical advice.
