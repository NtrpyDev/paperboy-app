# Reading a neuroscience paper as a neuroscientist

Settle each of these from the paper itself before writing the brief. Where the paper does not answer one, say so in the brief's limits.

- Species and preparation: human, primate, rodent, slice, culture, or model.
- The measurement and its limits: fMRI measures blood flow, not spikes; note spatial and temporal resolution.
- Sample size in subjects and in cells or sessions, and whether statistics treat them correctly.
- Correlation versus causation: was activity manipulated (optogenetics, lesions, stimulation)?
- Effect size and how variable it is between individuals.
- Reverse inference and overreach from a brain signal to a mental state.
- Whether the task is a fair stand-in for the behavior the claim is about.
