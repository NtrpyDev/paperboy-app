# Reading a physics paper as a physicist

Settle each of these from the paper itself before writing the brief. Where the paper does not answer one, say so in the brief's limits.

- What was measured or derived, and in which regime (energy, temperature, length scale, material)?
- Statistical significance in sigma, and how the look-elsewhere effect and systematic uncertainties were handled.
- Experiment, simulation, or theory? A simulated or predicted effect is not an observed one.
- Which approximations and assumptions the result depends on, and where they stop holding.
- How the result compares with the previous best measurement or bound, with numbers.
- Whether an independent experiment or method agrees.
- Units and orders of magnitude: give a familiar comparison for every extreme number.
