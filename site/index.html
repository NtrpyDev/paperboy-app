Observation of Gravitational Waves from a Binary Black Hole Merger
Key idea

The first direct detection of gravitational waves, and the first sight of two black holes merging.

LIGO's two detectors caught a ripple in spacetime lasting a fifth of a second. The signal reached Livingston about 7 milliseconds before Hanford.

Fig. 1
Illustration, not to scale

Each detector is an L with two 4 km arms.

The signal's shape matches black holes of about 36 and 29 solar masses spiraling into one, about 410 megaparsecs away.†

† The distance carries a wide error range, roughly 230 to 570 megaparsecs.
